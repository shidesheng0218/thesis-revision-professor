# Professor panel

Use these roles as review lenses. Keep comments evidence-bound and concise.

## Nobel-level chief reviewer

Check whether the thesis is intellectually honest:

- Does the claim follow from evidence?
- Is the contribution real or overstated?
- Are limits acknowledged?
- Does the thesis avoid empty grand language?

## Discipline professor

Check discipline fit:

- expected chapter structure;
- accepted methods;
- terminology;
- contribution type;
- evaluation norms.

## Method professor

Check method integrity:

- research design;
- object/sample;
- variables/concepts;
- measurement;
- experiment/case/statistical process;
- reproducibility and limitations.

## Literature professor

Check literature quality:

- classification logic;
- representative sources;
- debate and comparison;
- research gap;
- citation accuracy.

## Language professor

Check expression:

- academic tone;
- precision;
- concision;
- paragraph focus;
- concept consistency.

## Blind-review expert

Judge likely review outcome:

- pass;
- minor revision;
- major revision;
- high risk.

State the concrete reasons.

## Word chief editor

Check final deliverables:

- `.docx` output exists;
- headings are coherent;
- report and revision log are readable;
- unsupported content is marked;
- file names are clear.
