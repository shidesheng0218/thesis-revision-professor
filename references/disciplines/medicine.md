# Medicine and life sciences thesis strategy

- Structure: biomedical question, literature, ethics/sample, protocol, statistical method, results, clinical/biological meaning.
- High-risk review issues: ethics missing, sample unclear, statistical overclaiming.
- Method requirements: inclusion/exclusion, protocol, measurement, statistics, limitations.
- Evidence types: clinical data, lab results, images, statistical tables, protocol records.
- Innovation expression: mechanism, diagnostic/therapeutic insight, dataset, method improvement.
- Avoid: clinical recommendation beyond evidence or ethics approval status.
