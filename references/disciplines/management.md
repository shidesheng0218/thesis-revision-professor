# Management thesis strategy

- Structure: management problem, theory/model, hypotheses or questions, data/case, analysis, implication.
- High-risk review issues: variables undefined, model unsupported, case evidence thin.
- Method requirements: construct definition, data source, sample, empirical/case method, validity.
- Evidence types: survey data, interviews, firm cases, financial/operational indicators.
- Innovation expression: mechanism, context, managerial implication, model refinement.
- Avoid: consulting-style advice without analytical evidence.
