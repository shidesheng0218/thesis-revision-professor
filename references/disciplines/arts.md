# Arts thesis strategy

- Structure: creative/design problem, precedent analysis, method/process, work analysis, evaluation/reflection, conclusion.
- High-risk review issues: process undocumented, theory-practice disconnect, purely descriptive work.
- Method requirements: creative process, materials, iteration records, aesthetic/design rationale.
- Evidence types: works, sketches, process logs, user/viewer feedback, precedent cases.
- Innovation expression: form, process, material, interaction, cultural interpretation.
- Avoid: replacing analysis with personal preference statements.
