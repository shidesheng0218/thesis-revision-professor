# Economics thesis strategy

- Structure: economic question, theory/literature, model or empirical strategy, data, results, robustness, policy implication.
- High-risk review issues: identification weak, data source unclear, causal overclaiming.
- Method requirements: variable definitions, model specification, identification assumptions, robustness.
- Evidence types: statistical tables, datasets, policy documents, model derivation.
- Innovation expression: data, identification, mechanism, policy context, heterogeneity.
- Avoid: treating correlation as causation without design support.
