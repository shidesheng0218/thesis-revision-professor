# Education thesis strategy

- Structure: educational problem, literature/policy context, design or survey method, participants/materials, analysis, conclusion.
- High-risk review issues: sample unclear, intervention process vague, conclusion overgeneralized.
- Method requirements: participants, instruments, procedure, coding/statistics, ethics and limitations.
- Evidence types: questionnaires, interviews, classroom observations, learning analytics, policy documents.
- Innovation expression: teaching design, evaluation method, learner support, localized practice.
- Avoid: claiming universal teaching effects from small or unverified samples.
