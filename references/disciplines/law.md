# Law thesis strategy

- Structure: legal issue, normative framework, statutes/cases, doctrinal analysis, comparative or policy analysis, proposal.
- High-risk review issues: missing legal basis, normative leap, policy advice detached from doctrine.
- Method requirements: legal sources, interpretive method, case/statute comparison.
- Evidence types: laws, cases, regulations, official documents, scholarly commentary.
- Innovation expression: doctrinal clarification, institutional design, comparative insight.
- Avoid: empirical claims without data or legal proposals without normative basis.
