# Humanities thesis strategy

- Structure: question, material/text corpus, literature debate, conceptual framework, close analysis, conclusion.
- High-risk review issues: concept drift, textual evidence thin, literature debate absent.
- Method requirements: corpus/material boundary, interpretive framework, argument chain.
- Evidence types: primary texts, archives, historical materials, close readings, scholarly debate.
- Innovation expression: new interpretation, material reorganization, conceptual refinement.
- Avoid: broad cultural claims without textual or historical grounding.
