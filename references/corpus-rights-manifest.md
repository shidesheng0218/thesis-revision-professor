# Corpus rights manifest

Require explicit per-file authorization before deriving corpus strategies.

Each file entry must include:

- `relative_path`;
- `allow_derived_strategy`;
- lawful access or license basis recorded by the user;
- discipline, degree level, method, year band, and quality basis when known;
- retention or deletion obligation when applicable.

Do not treat public visibility as permission to scrape, store, or redistribute. Do not retain titles, authors, absolute paths, source paragraphs, or reconstructable embeddings in open-source outputs.

Only publish aggregate cards when the group size is at least `k=10`. Record `support_n`, slice, method, limitations, and quality basis. Frequency is not evidence of excellence.
