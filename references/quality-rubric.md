# Quality rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 5.

| Dimension | 0-1 | 2-3 | 4-5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Topic value | vague or trivial | relevant but broad | clear academic/practical value |
| Research question | absent or unstable | stated but not operational | precise and answerable |
| Literature review | list-like | categorized but shallow | critical, comparative, gap-driven |
| Theory/framework | absent | named but weakly connected | explains variables/concepts and analysis path |
| Method | unclear or invalid | generally usable but incomplete | appropriate, reproducible, bounded |
| Data/materials | missing | present but weakly described | sufficient and clearly documented |
| Analysis | descriptive only | partially supports claims | logically tied to question and evidence |
| Findings | unsupported or overclaimed | plausible but broad | bounded, evidenced, and useful |
| Innovation | fabricated or generic | present but weak | credible and discipline-appropriate |
| Language | informal or confusing | readable with problems | academic, precise, concise |
| Format | non-compliant | partially compliant | compliant and consistent |
| Citations | missing or inconsistent | usable but uneven | complete and traceable |

## Risk level

- **Pass**: no P0, few P1, claims are evidence-bound.
- **Minor revision**: no P0, several P1/P2.
- **Major revision**: at least one P0 or many P1 issues.
- **High risk**: unsupported findings, invalid method, serious citation gaps, or likely integrity problem.

## Master's baseline

A master's thesis should demonstrate the ability to define a problem, use a suitable method, analyze materials, and produce bounded conclusions.

## Doctoral baseline

A doctoral dissertation should demonstrate original contribution, deeper theoretical or methodological command, and more robust evidence.
