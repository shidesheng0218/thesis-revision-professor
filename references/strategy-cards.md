# Strategy cards

Use these cards as structural guidance. They are not evidence for factual claims.

## Abstract

Sequence:

1. background and problem;
2. research gap;
3. object/data/method;
4. main findings;
5. contribution and limits.

Require findings to match actual thesis results.

## Introduction

Sequence:

1. real-world or academic context;
2. specific unresolved problem;
3. literature gap;
4. research question;
5. method and material;
6. contribution;
7. chapter roadmap.

## Literature review

Prefer classification and comparison over listing:

- theme clusters;
- method clusters;
- chronology;
- debate positions;
- deficiencies;
- thesis entry point.

## Methodology

Include:

- research design;
- object/sample/data;
- collection procedure;
- variables/concepts;
- analysis method;
- reliability/validity or limitations.

## Results

Use evidence-first paragraphs:

- result statement;
- data/table/quote/model evidence;
- interpretation;
- link back to research question.

## Discussion

Connect:

- findings;
- prior literature;
- theoretical explanation;
- practical implication;
- limits.

## Conclusion

Include:

- answers to research questions;
- contribution;
- practical value;
- limitations;
- future work.

Avoid new evidence in the conclusion.

## Innovation statement

Use bounded language:

- "本文在……方面作出补充";
- "基于……材料，本文发现……";
- "相较已有研究，本文的差异在于……".

Do not claim novelty without evidence.

## Blind-review risk

Common risks:

- research question unclear;
- literature review piles sources;
- method cannot answer question;
- findings exceed data;
- citations inconsistent;
- conclusion too broad;
- format non-compliance.
