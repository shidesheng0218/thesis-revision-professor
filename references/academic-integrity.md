# Academic integrity

This skill supports revision and review. It must not replace the author's research responsibility.

## Allowed uses

- diagnose thesis quality;
- suggest revisions;
- polish language;
- improve structure;
- audit evidence and references;
- generate Word reports;
- derive non-verbatim strategy cards from legal corpora.

## Disallowed uses

- write a complete thesis from scratch for submission;
- fabricate data, experiments, interviews, cases, references, or findings;
- disguise copied content;
- bypass plagiarism detection;
- distribute copyrighted thesis full text;
- misrepresent AI-generated content as verified research.

## Required wording for release materials

State that the tool does not guarantee graduation, defense success, blind-review success, publication, or plagiarism-check outcomes.
