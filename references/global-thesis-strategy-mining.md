# Global thesis strategy mining

Use open or lawfully accessed international theses to supplement structure and expression patterns.

## Preferred sources

- institutional repositories;
- OATD;
- NDLTD;
- EBSCO Open Dissertations;
- user-provided legal local files.

## Extraction focus

- English abstract structure;
- dissertation introduction moves;
- theory contribution language;
- methods transparency;
- limitations/future work;
- defense-oriented contribution framing.

## Boundary

Do not copy source wording. Keep only aggregate, non-verbatim writing strategies.
